Background & Curiosity

About Me

First-Year ECE Student with a Focus on Silicon & Hardware

I am a first-year Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) student in Electronics and Communication Engineering at JECRC University, specializing in Semiconductor and Chip Design.

My journey began with a natural curiosity about what happens beneath the software layer: how electrons flow through silicon, how logic gates make mathematical decisions, and how microcontrollers interface with real-world physical systems.

Currently, I have introductory familiarity with prototyping platforms like Arduino, alongside early exposure to the ESP32 and Raspberry Pi ecosystem. I spend my study hours developing problem-solving skills, mastering foundational programming, and experimenting with basic circuits on a breadboard.

My long-term academic aim is to systematically learn semiconductor device physics, digital logic design, computer architecture, and modern VLSI design methodologies.
